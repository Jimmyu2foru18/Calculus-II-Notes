Key Formulas at a Glance

  • Ratio Test: $\rho = \lim_{n\to\infty} \left|\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}\right|$
  • Root Test: $\rho = \lim_{n\to\infty} \sqrt[n]{|a_n|}$
  • p-Series: $\sum \frac{1}{n^p}$ converges if $p > 1$
  • Geometric Series: $\sum ar^n = \frac{a}{1-r}$ for $|r| < 1$
  • Alternating Series: $\sum (-1)^{n+1}a_n$ converges if $a_n$ decreasing and $\lim a_n = 0$

Worked Examples

Example 1: Ratio Test

Task: Test $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{n!}{n^2}$

$$\rho = \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{(n+1)!/(n+1)^2}{n!/n^2}$$ $$= \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{(n+1)n!}{(n+1)^2} \cdot \frac{n^2}{n!}$$ $$= \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{n^2}{n+1} = \infty$$

Since $\rho = \infty > 1$, the series diverges.

Example 2: Power Series Interval of Convergence

Task: Find the interval of convergence for $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{n!}$

$$\rho = \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{|x|^{n+1}/(n+1)!}{|x|^n/n!}$$ $$= \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{|x|}{n+1} = 0$$

Since $\rho = 0 < 1$ for all $x$, the series converges for all $x$.

Interval of Convergence: $(-\infty, \infty)$

Radius of Convergence: $R = \infty$

Example 3: Alternating Series Test

Task: Test $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{n+1}}{n}$

$a_n = \frac{1}{n} > 0$ (TRUE)

$a_n$ is decreasing (TRUE)

$\lim_{n\to\infty} a_n = 0$ (TRUE)

All conditions satisfied. The series converges by the Alternating Series Test.
