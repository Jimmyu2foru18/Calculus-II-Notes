Key Formulas at a Glance

  • Polar to Rectangular: $x = r\cos(\theta)$, $y = r\sin(\theta)$
  • Rectangular to Polar: $r = \sqrt{x^2+y^2}$, $\theta = \tan^{-1}(y/x)$
  • Polar Area: $A = \frac{1}{2}\int_{\alpha}^{\beta} [r(\theta)]^2\,d\theta$
  • Parametric arc length: $L = \int_a^b \sqrt{[x'(t)]^2 + [y'(t)]^2}\,dt$
  • Taylor series: $f(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!}(x-a)^n$

Worked Examples

Example 1: Polar Coordinates

Task: Convert $r = 4\cos(\theta)$ to rectangular form

Click to see the solution

Multiply by $r$: $r^2 = 4r\cos(\theta)$

Substitute: $x^2 + y^2 = 4x$

Complete the square: $(x-2)^2 + y^2 = 4$

This is a circle of radius 2 centered at $(2, 0)$.

Example 2: Area in Polar Coordinates

Task: Find the area enclosed by $r = 1 + \sin(\theta)$

Click to see the solution $$A = \frac{1}{2}\int_0^{2\pi} (1+\sin(\theta))^2\,d\theta$$

Expand: $(1+\sin(\theta))^2 = 1 + 2\sin(\theta) + \sin^2(\theta)$

$$= 1 + 2\sin(\theta) + \frac{1-\cos(2\theta)}{2}$$

$$= \frac{3}{2} + 2\sin(\theta) - \frac{1}{2}\cos(2\theta)$$

$$A = \frac{1}{2}\left[\frac{3\theta}{2} - 2\cos(\theta) - \frac{\sin(2\theta)}{4}\right]_0^{2\pi}$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}(3\pi) = \frac{3\pi}{2}$$

Example 3: Parametric Arc Length

Task: Find the arc length of $x = t$, $y = t^2$ from $t=0$ to $t=1$

Click to see the solution

$\frac{dx}{dt} = 1$, $\frac{dy}{dt} = 2t$

$$L = \int_0^1 \sqrt{1 + 4t^2}\,dt$$

Let $2t = \tan(\theta)$, $dt = \frac{1}{2}\sec^2(\theta)\,d\theta$

$$L = \frac{1}{2}\int_0^{\arctan(2)} \sec^3(\theta)\,d\theta$$

$$= \frac{1}{2}\left[\frac{1}{2}\sec(\theta)\tan(\theta) + \frac{1}{2}\ln|\sec(\theta)+\tan(\theta)|\right]_0^{\arctan(2)}$$

$$= \frac{1}{4}\left[2\sqrt{5} + \ln(2+\sqrt{5})\right]$$
