Problem 1: Ratio Test

Task: Test the series sum from n=1 to infinity of n!/(n^2) for convergence.

Click to see the solution

Step 1: Apply the Ratio Test

rho = lim(n->infinity) |a_(n+1)/a_n|

a_n = n!/n^2

a_(n+1) = (n+1)!/(n+1)^2

Step 2: Compute the ratio

a_(n+1)/a_n = [(n+1)!/(n+1)^2] / [n!/n^2]

= [(n+1)*n!/(n+1)^2] * [n^2/n!]

= n^2/(n+1)

Step 3: Take the limit

rho = lim(n->infinity) n^2/(n+1) = infinity

Final Answer:

The series diverges by the Ratio Test (rho = infinity > 1).

Problem 2: Root Test

Task: Test the series sum from n=1 to infinity of (n/(n+1))^n for convergence.

Click to see the solution

Step 1: Apply the Root Test

rho = lim(n->infinity) |a_n|^(1/n)

a_n = (n/(n+1))^n

Step 2: Compute the nth root

|a_n|^(1/n) = [(n/(n+1))^n]^(1/n) = n/(n+1)

Step 3: Take the limit

rho = lim(n->infinity) n/(n+1) = 1

Final Answer:

The Root Test is inconclusive (rho = 1). Another test is needed.

Problem 3: Alternating Series Test

Task: Test the series sum from n=1 to infinity of (-1)^(n+1)/n for convergence.

Click to see the solution

Step 1: Check the conditions

a_n = 1/n

Condition 1: a_n > 0 for all n (TRUE)

Condition 2: a_n is decreasing (TRUE, since 1/(n+1) < 1/n)

Condition 3: lim(n->infinity) a_n = lim(n->infinity) 1/n = 0 (TRUE)

Step 2: Conclusion

All conditions are satisfied, so the series converges by the Alternating Series Test.

Final Answer:

The series converges by the Alternating Series Test.
