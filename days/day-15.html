Power Series and Convergence

Problem 1: Power Series - Interval of Convergence

Task: Find the interval and radius of convergence for sum from n=0 to infinity of x^n/n!

Click to see the solution

Step 1: Apply the Ratio Test

rho = lim(n->infinity) |x^(n+1)/(n+1)!| / |x^n/n!|

= lim(n->infinity) |x|/(n+1)

Step 2: Determine convergence

For any x, lim(n->infinity) |x|/(n+1) = 0 < 1

So the series converges for all x.

Final Answer:

Interval of Convergence: (-infinity, infinity)

Radius of Convergence: R = infinity

Problem 2: Power Series - Finite Radius

Task: Find the interval of convergence for sum from n=1 to infinity of x^n/n

Click to see the solution

Step 1: Apply the Ratio Test

rho = lim(n->infinity) |x^(n+1)/(n+1)| / |x^n/n|

= lim(n->infinity) |x| * n/(n+1) = |x|

Step 2: Find where rho < 1

|x| < 1, so -1 < x < 1

Step 3: Test endpoints

At x = 1: sum of 1/n (harmonic series) diverges.

At x = -1: sum of (-1)^n/n converges (alternating harmonic series).

Final Answer:

Interval of Convergence: [-1, 1)

Radius of Convergence: R = 1
