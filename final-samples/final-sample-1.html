Problem 1: Integration by Parts

Evaluate: $\int x^2 e^x\,dx$

First integration by parts: $u = x^2$, $dv = e^x dx$

$du = 2x\,dx$, $v = e^x$

$$= x^2 e^x - 2\int x e^x\,dx$$

Second integration by parts: $u = x$, $dv = e^x dx$

$du = dx$, $v = e^x$

$$\int x e^x\,dx = x e^x - \int e^x\,dx = x e^x - e^x$$

Substitute back:

$$= x^2 e^x - 2(x e^x - e^x) + C$$ $$= (x^2 - 2x + 2)e^x + C$$

Problem 2: Series Convergence

Task: Test $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{n+1}}{n}$ for convergence

This is the alternating harmonic series.

$a_n = \frac{1}{n} > 0$

$a_n$ is decreasing

$\lim_{n\to\infty} a_n = 0$

All conditions of the Alternating Series Test are satisfied.

The series converges.

Problem 3: Volume by Shells

Task: Find the volume when the region bounded by $y = x^2$ and $y = 4$ is revolved about the y-axis.

Intersection: $x^2 = 4 \implies x = 2$

$$V = 2\pi \int_0^2 x(4-x^2)\,dx$$ $$= 2\pi \int_0^2 (4x - x^3)\,dx$$ $$= 2\pi \left[2x^2 - \frac{x^4}{4}\right]_0^2$$ $$= 2\pi(8 - 4)$$ $$= 8\pi$$
