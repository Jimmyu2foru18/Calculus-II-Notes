Quick Reference

  • $\int x^n\,dx = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} + C$
  • $\int e^{ax}\,dx = \frac{1}{a}e^{ax} + C$
  • $\int \sin(ax)\,dx = -\frac{1}{a}\cos(ax) + C$
  • $\int \cos(ax)\,dx = \frac{1}{a}\sin(ax) + C$

Practice Checklist
