Worked Examples

Example 1: FTC Part II

Evaluate: $\int_0^2 (3x^2 + 2x)\,dx$

Click to see the solution

Step 1:

Find the antiderivative: $F(x) = x^3 + x^2$.

Step 2:

Apply FTC Part II:

$F(2) - F(0) = (8 + 4) - (0 + 0)$

Final Answer:

$$12$$

Example 2: FTC Part I

Find: $\frac{d}{dx}\int_0^x \sin(t^2)\,dt$

Click to see the solution

Step 1:

Apply FTC Part I: $\frac{d}{dx}\int_a^x f(t)\,dt = f(x)$.

Final Answer:

$$\sin(x^2)$$

Example 3: Variable Upper Limit

Find: $\frac{d}{dx}\int_1^{x^2} e^t\,dt$

Click to see the solution

Step 1:

Apply the variable upper limit rule: $\frac{d}{dx}\int_a^{g(x)} f(t)\,dt = f(g(x))\cdot g'(x)$.

Step 2:

Here $f(t) = e^t$, $g(x) = x^2$, and $g'(x) = 2x$.

Final Answer:

$$e^{x^2} \cdot 2x$$

Example 4: Mean Value Theorem for Integrals

Find the average value: Of $f(x) = x^2$ on $[1, 3]$

Click to see the solution

Step 1:

Compute the integral:

$\int_1^3 x^2\,dx = \left[\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_1^3 = \frac{27}{3} - \frac{1}{3} = \frac{26}{3}$

Step 2:

Apply the average value formula:

$f_{\text{avg}} = \frac{1}{b-a}\int_a^b f(x)\,dx = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{26}{3}$

Final Answer:

$$\frac{13}{3}$$
