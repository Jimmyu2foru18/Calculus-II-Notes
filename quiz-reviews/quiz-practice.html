Key Formulas

FormulaDescription
$\int x^n\,dx = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} + C$Power Rule ($n \neq -1$)
$\int e^{ax}\,dx = \frac{1}{a}e^{ax} + C$Exponential

Worked Examples

Example 1: u-Substitution

Evaluate: $\int x e^{x^2}\,dx$

Click to see the solution

Step 1:

Let $u = x^2$, $du = 2x\,dx$, so $x\,dx = \frac{du}{2}$

Step 2:

Substitute into the integral:

$$= \int e^u \cdot \frac{du}{2}$$

Step 3:

Integrate and back-substitute:

$$= \frac{1}{2}e^u + C$$

Final Answer:

$$\frac{1}{2}e^{x^2} + C$$

Example 2: Trig Integral

Evaluate: $\int_0^{\pi} \sin^2(x)\,dx$

Click to see the solution

Step 1:

Use the identity $\sin^2(x) = \frac{1-\cos(2x)}{2}$

Step 2:

Substitute and integrate:

$$= \int_0^{\pi} \frac{1-\cos(2x)}{2}\,dx$$

Step 3:

Evaluate the antiderivative:

$$= \frac{1}{2}\left[x - \frac{\sin(2x)}{2}\right]_0^{\pi}$$

Final Answer:

$$= \frac{1}{2}(\pi - 0) = \frac{\pi}{2}$$

Example 3: Area Between Curves

Find the area: Between $y = x^2$ and $y = x+2$

Click to see the solution

Step 1:

Find intersection points: $x^2 = x+2 \implies x^2-x-2 = 0 \implies (x+1)(x-2) = 0$

So $x = -1$ and $x = 2$

Step 2:

Set up the area integral (line is above the parabola on $[-1, 2]$):

$$A = \int_{-1}^2 [(x+2) - x^2]\,dx$$

Step 3:

Evaluate the antiderivative:

$$= \left[\frac{x^2}{2} + 2x - \frac{x^3}{3}\right]_{-1}^2$$

Final Answer:

$$= \left(2 + 4 - \frac{8}{3}\right) - \left(\frac{1}{2} - 2 + \frac{1}{3}\right) = \frac{9}{2}$$
